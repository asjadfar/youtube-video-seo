# Description Template — Long-Form Video

Fill in every bracket. No placeholder is ever published. The first
2 lines carry the main keyword and the promise — that is the part
viewers see before "Show more". Then what the video teaches,
timestamps that match the real video, links the owner approved,
and 2–3 hashtags at the end.

Target length: 200–400 characters for the text before the timestamps
is a good length for most videos; at least 100 characters in total.
Score the finished description with `scripts/check_seo.py`.

## Checklist for this description

- [ ] Main keyword in the first 2 lines
- [ ] Promise in the first 2 lines — what the viewer will learn
- [ ] Learning goal written in plain words, matching the real video
- [ ] Timestamps checked against the real video (first one is 0:00,
      at least 3 timestamps, at least 10 seconds apart)
- [ ] Links only as the owner approved — no link that was not given
- [ ] 2–3 hashtags maximum, at the end
- [ ] At least 100 characters, 200–400 is a good length
- [ ] No keyword stuffing, no claim the video does not deliver
