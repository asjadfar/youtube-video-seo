# Title Formula Sheet

Pick the formula that fits the video, then fill it in with the real main
keyword and the real learning goal. Every title must still pass the
rules in `SKILL.md` (keyword-first, under 60 characters where possible,
never over 100) and score 80 or above in `scripts/check_seo.py`.
Write 3 title options for every video and let the owner pick one.

## How to use a formula

1. Take the main keyword from Step 2 of `SKILL.md` (for example
   "count 1 to 10 for kids").
2. Pick one formula below. Do not mix two formulas in one title.
3. Fill in the brackets with the real topic. Keep the keyword at or
   near the start.
4. Count the characters. If it is over 60, shorten the second half
   first — never cut the keyword.
5. Score all 3 options with the checker before the approval gate.

## The formulas

### 1. Keyword + "with Bunny" (the channel's standard formula)

Use for: most Bunny Sounds videos. The learning goal is the keyword,
and Bunny is the host.

Pattern: `[Keyword] with Bunny | [What the viewer learns]`

Examples:
- Count 1 to 10 for Kids with Bunny | Numbers (46)
- Learn Colors with Bunny | Colors for Toddlers (46)
- Animal Sounds with Bunny | Cow, Lion & Duck (45)

### 2. Question the viewer wants answered

Use for: a video that answers one clear question, usually an animal
sound or a "what" question. The question is the curiosity; the second
half is the clarity.

Pattern: `[Question]? | [Keyword]`

Examples:
- What Sound Does a Cow Make? | Animal Sounds for Kids (55)
- What Sound Does a Lion Make? | Animal Sounds for Kids (56)
- What Color Is the Sky? | Learn Colors for Kids (44)

### 3. "Learn [topic] for [audience]"

Use for: a straight teaching video where the audience matters — a
parent searching for their child's age group.

Pattern: `Learn [Topic] for [Audience] | [Keyword]`

Examples:
- Learn Colors for Toddlers | Red, Blue & Yellow with Bunny (58)
- Learn the Letter A for Kids | ABC with Bunny (44)
- Learn Numbers for Toddlers | Count 1 to 10 with Bunny (53)

### 4. Number or list in the title

Use for: counting, letters, or a set of things the video covers.
The number tells the viewer exactly how much they will learn.

Pattern: `[Keyword] | [Number] [Things] with Bunny`

Examples:
- Count 1 to 10 for Kids | 10 Numbers with Bunny (47)
- Animal Sounds for Kids | 6 Animals with Bunny (45)
- Learn 5 Colors for Toddlers | Red, Blue, Green & More (54)

### 5. "Fun" + keyword (for Shorts)

Use for: Shorts, where the title is short and punchy. Keyword first,
then at most a few more words. Do not use this formula for long-form
— it is too thin on its own there.

Pattern: `[Keyword] | Fun [Topic] with Bunny`

Examples:
- Count 1 to 10 for Kids | Fun with Bunny (39)
- Animal Sounds | Fun with Bunny (28)
- Learn Colors | Fun Colors with Bunny (36)

### 6. How-to / tutorial (general channels)

Use for: a general (non-kids) tutorial video, for example a Shopify
or how-to video. Same rules: keyword first, under 60 characters,
one idea per title.

Pattern: `[Keyword] | [What the viewer gets]`

Examples:
- Shopify Store Design for Beginners | Full Tutorial (54)
- How to Count 1 to 10 | Numbers Song for Kids (44)
- Learn the ABC Song | Letters for Toddlers (41)

## Title rules — quick check

- [ ] Main keyword at or near the start
- [ ] Under 60 characters where possible, never over 100
- [ ] Says clearly what the video teaches (clarity) and gives a
      reason to click (curiosity)
- [ ] One idea only — no second topic crammed in
- [ ] No ALL CAPS, no more than one exclamation mark, at most one emoji
- [ ] No clickbait the video does not deliver, no unrelated trending
      keyword, no keyword stuffing
- [ ] 3 options written, all scored with `scripts/check_seo.py`,
      owner picks one

## Worked example — Episode 1, "Count 1 to 10 with Bunny"

Main keyword: count 1 to 10 for kids

| Option | Title | Chars | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Count 1 to 10 for Kids with Bunny | Numbers | 46 | 1 |
| B | Count 1 to 10 for Kids | Fun Numbers with Bunny | 46 | 3 |
| C | Count 1 to 10 for Kids | Fun with Bunny | 39 | 5 (Shorts cut) |

Option A is the pick for the long-form video: keyword first, the
bunny's name, the learning goal in plain words, 44 characters.
Option C is the pick if the same lesson is cut as a 60-second Short.
